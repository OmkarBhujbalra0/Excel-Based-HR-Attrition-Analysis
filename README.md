# Excel-Based-HR-Attrition-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Excel to identify key factors influencing employee turnover. The goal is to transform raw HR data into actionable insights through structured analysis, pivot tables, charts, and an interactive dashboard.

## Objective

To understand:

- Overall attrition rate
- Departments with higher attrition risk
- Impact of overtime on attrition
- Relationship between job satisfaction, work–life balance, and attrition

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 35 employee-related columns, including:

Numerical Features:
Age, Monthly Income, Years at Company, Years at Current Role, Total Working Hours, etc.

Categorical Features:
Attrition, Department, Job Role, Gender, OverTime, Marital Status, etc.

Ordinal Features:
Job Satisfaction, Work Life Balance, Environment Satisfaction, Job Level, Performance Rating, etc.

## Tools & Techniques Used

Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers (Department-based filtering)

## Key KPIs

- Total Employees
- Total Employees Left
- Attrition Percentage

## Key Analysis Performed

- Department vs Attrition
- Overtime vs Attrition
- Job Satisfaction vs Attrition


## Key Insights

- Overall attrition rate is approximately 16%
- Sales department shows higher attrition compared to others
- Employees working overtime are nearly 3x more likely to leave
- Lower job satisfaction and poor work–life balance significantly increase attrition risk
- Employees working overtime with low job satisfaction are the most vulnerable group

## Recommendations

- Introduce better work–life balance policies, especially for Sales teams
- Monitor and limit excessive overtime
- Implement strategies to improve job satisfaction
- Focus retention efforts on high-risk employee groups

## Conclusion

Employee attrition is strongly influenced by overtime, job satisfaction, and work–life balance. By addressing workload management and employee well-being, organizations can reduce attrition and improve long-term workforce stability.
